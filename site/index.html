WHY CBDES / 01

Why is a new approach to autonomous driving development needed?

From algorithm design and software integration to deployment and on-board verification, R&D must connect multiple stages. How can mature capabilities be reused, and how can each round of verification drive the next iteration of development?

01SAFETY VERIFICATION

Complex Safety Verification

With diverse scenarios and long execution chains, algorithm modifications require continuous verification across both simulation and on-board deployment.

Need to bridge development and verification
02DEVELOPMENT COST

High Development Costs

Differences in interfaces and platforms lead to redundant development, making it difficult to directly reuse mature algorithms.

Need to build up reusable capabilities
03ITERATION EFFICIENCY

Low Iteration Efficiency

Modeling, coding, deployment, and feedback are fragmented, making it difficult to quickly close the loop on modifications.

Need to seamlessly connect each round of iteration

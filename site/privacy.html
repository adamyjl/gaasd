Privacy & analytics

To understand how visitors use the site and its development demos, we record visit times, IP addresses, browser and operating system details, and video playback, including watch time and progress.

An offline IP database estimates locations, usually to country, region, or city level. We do not collect GPS coordinates, and these estimates do not identify a precise home address. Data is stored on GAASD's Tencent Cloud server. Detailed reports require administrator access.

Temporary identifiers in the current browser tab link visits and video playback. We do not use persistent visitor-tracking cookies or send these events to third-party analytics platforms.

This preference applies to future page and playback events from this browser. The server still keeps basic access logs needed to operate the website. Browser Do Not Track settings also disable event reporting.
